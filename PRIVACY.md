# Privacy

EVE-O Preview — personal build does not collect telemetry, analytics, crash reports or personal data, and does not send user data to the maintainer.

- Profiles, settings, preview layouts, caches and logs are stored locally on your computer.
- The application may read local EVE Online client files and logs to show previews, identity information and combat-log augments. These files stay on your computer.
- The application may request public EVE data, such as character names, portraits and static data, from CCP services (`esi.evetech.net`, `images.evetech.net` and the EVE developers static-data service). It may send a public character ID to resolve a name. It does not read or transmit EVE account passwords or authentication tokens.
- The application contains links to GitHub, Discord and documentation. Opening a link uses your browser and is subject to that site's privacy policy.
- Release binaries are submitted to the [SignPath Foundation](https://signpath.org/) for code signing. SignPath processes the build artifact, not your local profiles or game data.

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue in this repository.
